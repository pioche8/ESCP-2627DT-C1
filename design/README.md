# design/

The designer's handoffs and reference pages, kept as they were delivered, plus `figma/`, the scripts that copy the built app into Figma. **This is reference material and dev tooling, not app code:** nothing here is loaded by the app, and the website's build only copies photos from `assets/`. What ships today is described in [`../UI_DESIGN.md`](../UI_DESIGN.md), and its colours, type and radius come from [`../shared/tokens.json`](../shared/tokens.json).

**Which source wins** (from [`../docs/START_HERE.md`](../docs/START_HERE.md) §3): [`../DESIGN.md`](../DESIGN.md), then `HANDOFF_V2.md` and `MOTION.md` where they differ, then the 4 Nov declutter decisions in [`../CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md) §11 items 53–68. The engine and the data win over all of them. So several things drawn here were later removed from the app on purpose: the Sky and Harbor palettes (item 54), pills, glass, gradients and the mono typeface (items 53–66). Those removals are about the app, and they still hold there. The website follows the same decluttered rules (items 53–68) and, since 7 Nov, plays the motion of its original 3 Nov design in solid colours ([ADR-0004](../docs/adr/0004-website-current-look-original-motion.md), `CLAUDE.md` §11 items 164–167; items 26–40 say where it departs from the handoff): the glows, gradients, glass pills and mono typeface drawn in `website/` stay out of both products.

**Viewing the reference pages:** serve the folder (for example `python3 -m http.server`) and open a `.dc.html` file. They load React, Babel and Google Fonts from CDNs, so they need internet; the app itself does not.

`DESIGN.md` §11 lists files from the designer's original bundle (`TenderScout Renders.dc.html`, `TenderScout 3D.html`, `design_handoff_tenderscout_bold/`, `archive/`) that were never part of this repository.
