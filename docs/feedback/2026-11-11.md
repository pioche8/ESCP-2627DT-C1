**Q2 · Evidence**
> The course counts a survey as an interview, so our survey responses are our stakeholder interviews, each labelled as survey. How many responses per team member do you expect for the submission?

Context, if he asks: stakeholder evidence of 2 responses, collected by survey, 1 in our 5–50 segment; the gate is signed "Provisional, n = 2"; the persona, HMW statements and journey are drafts built on survey response S1, every line citing a survey response or marked assumption.

*What we do:*

- He wants interviews as well: the call with the respondent of survey response S1 is booked first (it also asks willingness to pay, which the form never did); surveys stay labelled as survey.
- He does not accept surveys: the gate goes back to unsigned, and the persona, HMW and journey are relabelled assumption-based until interviews exist.

**Q3 · A real tender read by a real model** (word for word from the decision record)
> "Would a single, clearly labelled real tender read by a model, on the website only, off by default, strengthen the submission, or does the course want the AI simulated throughout?"

Let him answer first. Unless he clearly says "simulated throughout", give the cost in one sentence (the preconditions of the decision record): building it means changing our "no live AI" rule, a check that the app safely displays text it did not write, putting the website online, and a team member who owns the model key and its spending cap. Options: (a) simulated throughout, (b) it stays a documented decision, not built, (c) we build it, before or after the persona and journey work.

*What we do:* (a) the decision record is closed as rejected; (b), or no ruling, it stays proposed with his answer quoted; (c) a team decision amends the rule, then the safety check, the website online and the key owner, ranked against the persona and journey work by the team (about 5 days, low confidence).
